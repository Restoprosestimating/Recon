What the workbook does

  • Reads Internal TAM labor-breakdown hours and recosts them at supervisor / worker gross rates plus burden.
  • Carries Xactimate material and equipment totals into budget sheets you can overwrite with actuals.
  • Applies royalty to the negotiated price (top box on Summary) and computes gross profit and margin.
  • Gives you variance columns and an invoice register for any Restopros job.
